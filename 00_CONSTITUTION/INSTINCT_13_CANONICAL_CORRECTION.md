# INSTINCT 13 — Canonical Concept Correction

**Status:** Authoritative correction / supersedes conflicting legacy descriptions  
**Scope:** Core concept, twelve-part structure, symbolism and visual identity  
**Source provenance:** User Library document `INSTINCT_13_CANONICAL_CORRECTION.md`, created 2026-10-31. Updated with project-owner decisions and terminology clarification on 2026-10-31.

## Terminology: archetypes and manifestations

The project owner has confirmed that the **twelve are archetypes**: recurring patterns of human experience. Each archetype can manifest in different ways depending on the person, context, experiences and moment in time.

- **Archetype:** the underlying recurring pattern.
- **Manifestation:** a particular expression of that pattern in a given context.
- **Person:** is not an archetype and must not be permanently classified by one.
- **Animal:** is a symbolic representation of an archetype, not the archetype itself and not a manifestation.

One archetype may have multiple manifestations. The system observes patterns and their expressions; it does not classify people.

The Decision Log dated 2026-08-05 records: **“Approved term 'Manifestation' replacing 'Archetype'.”** That historical entry is preserved and must not be silently rewritten. Its wording now requires explicit reconciliation with the confirmed distinction above.

## 1. The 13 is not one of the twelve

The number **13 is not an archetype, character, animal, personality, role or named entity**.

It is a **pure manifestation**, outside the twelve.

It has:
- no name;
- no animal representation;
- no human or anthropomorphic form;
- no gender or personal identity;
- no fixed personality, virtue, vice or instinct;
- no membership in either opposing side.

The 13 must not be depicted as a butterfly, moth, human figure, animal, awakened character or any other identifiable being. It must not be assigned a descriptive title such as “The Awakened”.

The 13 may be indicated only through abstract presence or manifestation. It is not another participant in the twelve-part structure.

## 2. The twelve archetypes

The system contains **twelve archetypes arranged as six opposing pairs**. Each archetype can have multiple contextual manifestations. Animals are symbolic representations of the archetypes, not the archetypes themselves.

The project owner confirmed that **The Performer and the transformation concept are opposites**. The transformation concept must not be named after its animal: the animal is only one possible metaphor and may change independently of the underlying concept.

The project owner's current conceptual direction for this position:
- limitations are often internal to the individual;
- learning and experience produce continuous evolution;
- transformation does not erase the past or rebuild from absolute zero; it incorporates and reorganizes what came before;
- a change of perspective can reveal a different meaning or possibility without requiring external reality to change;
- transformation is not necessarily a moral improvement, a superior state, or a predetermined final form.

**The Becoming** is currently a candidate name only. It has not been approved as the final name. The animal representation is also undecided.

The legacy *Circle of Instincts* image associated a butterfly with the Performer's opposite and incorrectly placed “The Awakened” at position 13. The butterfly may remain a historical visual metaphor, but it is not the name of the transformation concept and cannot represent the 13th.

## 3. Structural rule

- Six archetypes occupy one side of the opposition structure.
- Six occupy the opposing side.
- The twelve form six opposing pairs.
- Each archetype may manifest in different ways depending on context.
- The 13th is outside the pairs and outside both sides.
- The 13th has no fixed position within the twelve-part structure and no animal or character portrait.
- Concepts, names, pairings and animal symbols may be reviewed independently; changes must be deliberate and documented.

## 4. Red is a pulse, not a permanent brand colour

The permanent visual foundation is:
- **Black:** primary field, depth and negative space.
- **White / ivory:** legibility and editorial typography.
- **Gold / bronze:** structure, fine geometry and restrained material accents.

**Red is not a constant highlight colour.** It appears only when a pulse / manifestation occurs. It should be brief, deliberate and meaningful—not used as a permanent border, background, decorative stripe or routine accent.

## 5. Visual rules for the 13th manifestation

Do not render the 13th as:
- a butterfly or moth;
- a thirteenth animal;
- a human face or body;
- a gendered figure;
- a named or titled character;
- a fixed emblem that implies a personality.

If the 13th is suggested visually, use absence, a transient pulse, a momentary change in light, a subtle disruption of the composition, or another non-figurative event. The manifestation should not become a character.

## 6. Correction to legacy material

Any legacy text or artwork that labels the 13th as **“The Awakened”**, assigns it the butterfly/moth, or gives it a fixed character form is superseded by this correction for all future I13 work.

The legacy artwork may remain in the archive as historical material, but it is not the authority for the corrected concept. Future diagrams, wallpapers, videos, presentations and written materials must follow this document.

## 7. Core statement

**Twelve archetypes form six opposing pairs. Each represents a recurring pattern of human experience and can manifest in different ways. Animals are changeable symbolic representations. The 13th is outside the twelve: pure manifestation, nameless, formless, and belonging to neither side.**
