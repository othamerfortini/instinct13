# INSTINCT 13 — Canonical Concept Correction

**Status:** Authoritative correction / supersedes conflicting legacy descriptions  
**Scope:** Core concept, archetype structure, symbolism and visual identity  
**Source provenance:** User Library document `INSTINCT_13_CANONICAL_CORRECTION.md`, created 2026-10-31. Updated with the project owner's explicit confirmation of the sixth opposing pair on 2026-10-31.

## 1. The 13 is not an archetype

The number **13 is not an archetype, character, animal, personality, role or named entity**.

It is a **pure manifestation**.

It has:
- no name;
- no animal representation;
- no human or anthropomorphic form;
- no gender or personal identity;
- no fixed personality, virtue, vice or instinct;
- no membership in either opposing side.

The 13 must not be depicted as a butterfly, moth, human figure, animal, awakened character or any other identifiable being. It must not be assigned a descriptive archetype title such as “The Awakened”.

The 13 may be indicated only through abstract presence or manifestation. It is not another participant in the system; it is not one of the twelve.

## 2. The twelve archetypes

The system contains **twelve animal archetypes arranged as six opposing pairs**:

| Side A | Opposing side B |
|---|---|
| The Manipulator — Fox | The Scholar — Owl |
| The Ego — Lion | The Echo — Stag / Deer |
| The Consumer — Pig | The Liberated — Eagle |
| The Follower — Sheep | The Architect — Spider |
| The Performer — Peacock | **The Butterfly — Butterfly** |
| The Predator — Wolf | The Collector — Raven / Crow |

The project owner explicitly confirmed that **The Performer (Peacock) and The Butterfly (Butterfly) are opposites**. The Butterfly symbolizes transformation: the movement from the ugly to the beautiful, and the emergence of a new form through metamorphosis. The Performer represents outward display and the pursuit of external recognition. These are opposing manifestations, not a moral ranking.

The legacy *Circle of Instincts* image incorrectly associated “The Awakened” and the butterfly with position 13. That legacy association is superseded: **The Butterfly is the twelfth archetype; the 13th remains formless and is not a butterfly.**

## 3. Structural rule

- Six archetypes occupy one side of the opposition structure.
- Six archetypes occupy the opposing side.
- The twelve form six opposing pairs.
- The 13th is outside the pairs and outside both sides.
- The 13th has no fixed position as an archetype and no animal or character portrait.

## 4. Red is a pulse, not a permanent brand colour

The permanent visual foundation is:
- **Black:** primary field, depth and negative space.
- **White / ivory:** legibility and editorial typography.
- **Gold / bronze:** structure, fine geometry and restrained material accents.

**Red is not a constant highlight colour.** It appears only when a pulse / manifestation occurs. It should be brief, deliberate and meaningful—not used as a permanent border, background, decorative stripe or routine accent.

## 5. Visual rules for the 13th manifestation

Do not render the 13th as:
- a butterfly or moth;
- a thirteenth animal;
- a human face or body;
- a gendered figure;
- a named or titled character;
- a fixed emblem that implies a personality.

If the 13th is suggested visually, use absence, a transient pulse, a momentary change in light, a subtle disruption of the composition, or another non-figurative event. The manifestation should not become a character.

## 6. Correction to legacy material

Any legacy text or artwork that labels the 13th as **“The Awakened”**, assigns it the butterfly/moth, or gives it a fixed character form is superseded by this correction for all future I13 work.

The legacy artwork may remain in the archive as historical material, but it is not the authority for the corrected concept. Future diagrams, wallpapers, videos, presentations and written materials must follow this document.

## 7. Core statement

**Twelve archetypes are arranged as six opposing pairs. The Butterfly opposes The Performer. The thirteenth is not an archetype. It is pure manifestation: nameless, formless, and belonging to neither side.**
