**Classification:** Constitutional  
**Status:** Active  
**Authority:** The Constitution  
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

This protocol defines the permanent responsibilities, authority boundaries, and collaboration model of every artificial intelligence system participating in the development of the Instinct 13 universe.

Its purpose is to preserve constitutional identity while enabling efficient implementation, research, validation, and deployment.

No AI possesses architectural authority independently.

All authority derives from the Constitution.

---

# Constitutional Principle

Artificial intelligence systems are collaborators.

They are never constitutional authorities.

Every architectural decision remains subordinate to the Constitution.

Every implementation remains subordinate to the approved architecture.

---

# Source of Authority

The order of authority is immutable unless modified through constitutional amendment.

1. Constitution
2. Canon
3. Architectural Baseline
4. Experience Architecture
5. Information Architecture
6. Visual Language
7. Implementation

No AI may reverse this order.

# Non-overlap Principle

Responsibilities shall never overlap.

ChatGPT does not implement.

GitHub Copilot does not design architecture.

Perplexity does not make constitutional decisions.

Vercel does not modify implementation.

---

# Escalation Rule

Whenever implementation conflicts with architecture:

1. Stop implementation.

2. Produce a technical report.

3. Identify the architectural conflict.

4. Await constitutional review.

No implementation may resolve constitutional uncertainty independently.

---

# Implementation Principle

Implementation exists to validate architecture.

Implementation never defines architecture.

---

# Research Principle

Research informs decisions.

Research never becomes constitutional authority.

Evidence supports architecture.

Evidence does not replace governance.

---

# Deployment Principle

A successful deployment validates implementation.

It never validates constitutional correctness.

---

# Success Criteria

The collaboration model is considered successful when:

- Constitutional identity remains preserved.
- Architecture remains coherent.
- Implementation remains faithful.
- Deployments remain stable.
- Research remains evidence-based.
- Responsibilities remain clearly separated.

# Constitutional Closing Statement

The purpose of AI governance is not to increase automation.
