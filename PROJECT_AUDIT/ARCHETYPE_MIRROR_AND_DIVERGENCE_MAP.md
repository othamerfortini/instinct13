# Instinct 13 — Mirror and Divergence Map of the Twelve Archetypes

**Status:** Exploratory analysis; not owner-approved canon.  
**Branch:** `docs/restore-canonical-i13-sources`  
**Purpose:** Show where the twelve archetypes share human qualities (mirror) and where their emphases may differ (divergence), within each pair and across the full system. This is an identification map, not a classification tool or set of exclusive definitions.

## 1. How to read this map

- **Mirror:** a quality, capacity, impulse or behaviour that may appear through more than one archetype.
- **Divergence:** a possible difference in emphasis, orientation, context or expression. It is not a hard boundary.
- **Manifestation:** a concrete expression in a particular situation. The same person may express several archetypes in one event, or different ones over time.
- **Overlap is expected:** a quality appearing in two archetypes does not make them duplicates, and it does not mean the quality belongs exclusively to either one.
- **No moral ranking:** neither side of a pair is inherently better, worse, healthier, more evolved or more authentic.

Where the owner has supplied a clear signal, it is marked as owner-grounded. Other distinctions are hypotheses to test, not established meanings.

## 2. The six pairs: where they mirror and where they may diverge

### Pair 1 — The Manipulator / Fox ↔ The Scholar / Owl

**Owner-grounded starting points**
- Fox: astuteness / cunning can be constructive or beneficial, not inherently negative.
- Owl: profound or encompassing knowledge, symbolically expressed by the owner as “the owl that knows everything”.
- The owner rejected the earlier assistant-created contrast of control / influence versus understanding. That axis must not return as the pair's definition.

**Where they mirror**
- Intelligence and perception.
- Observation and alertness.
- Anticipation.
- Learning from experience.
- Recognizing patterns or openings.
- Reading a situation beyond its most obvious surface.

**Where they may diverge — tentative**
- Fox may draw attention to *astuteness in navigating a particular situation*: noticing an opening, adapting, improvising, finding a workable route.
- Owl may draw attention to *depth or breadth of knowledge*: integrating what is known, seeing context, understanding connections and drawing on accumulated learning.

This is a difference in emphasis, not kind or exclusivity. A clever route can depend on deep knowledge; profound knowledge can be applied with great astuteness.

**Example of overlap**
Someone understands a complex situation, notices a possibility others missed and devises an ingenious response. Both Fox-like and Owl-like qualities may be present.

**Potential false distinction to avoid**
Do not say Fox acts while Owl only knows; both can act. Do not say Fox is cunning and Owl is wise as if cunning cannot contain wisdom or wisdom cannot be astute. Do not make Fox morally suspect.

**Current assessment**
The animal symbols have owner-grounded starting points. The inherited labels remain semantically under review: “Manipulator” may distort positive astuteness, while “Scholar” may be narrower than encompassing knowledge. The conceptual relation remains open.

### Pair 2 — The Ego / Lion ↔ The Echo / Stag or Deer

**Evidence status**
The names and animals are inherited working material. The owner has not yet supplied a detailed definition or confirmed the proposed axis below.

**Where they may mirror**
- Both may concern identity as experienced in relation to others.
- Both may involve recognition: being seen, seeing oneself reflected, or noticing how one is received.
- Both may be shaped by social environments and relationships.
- Both may involve sensitivity to respect, acceptance, rejection or belonging.

**Where they may diverge — tentative**
- Lion / Ego may emphasize *self-reference and self-assertion*: one's own identity, dignity, presence, agency, boundaries or desire for recognition.
- Echo may emphasize *resonance and reflection*: receiving, mirroring, carrying, repeating or being shaped by voices, signals and responses from outside oneself.

**Example of overlap**
A person speaks confidently in a group but adjusts their message after sensing the group's response. Self-assertion and social resonance can coexist in the same moment.

**Potential false distinction to avoid**
Do not reduce Lion to selfishness or Echo to weakness / imitation. Selfhood is partly formed in relationships; responsiveness to others does not erase agency.

**Animal / naming question**
The Lion readily communicates presence, pride and authority. Stag / Deer may communicate alertness, sensitivity or group awareness, but does not immediately communicate echo or mirroring. If “Echo” remains central, its animal representation needs review. If Deer remains central, the intended pattern may need a different name.

**Current assessment**
This pair has a potentially useful self-reference / external-resonance tension, but it is unconfirmed. The conceptual axis, name and animal match all remain open.

### Pair 3 — The Consumer / Pig ↔ The Liberated / Eagle

**Evidence status**
The Pig eating fast food is an owner-supplied example of the original visual concept. The wider pair interpretation remains exploratory.

**Where they may mirror**
- Both may concern a person's relationship with desire and choice.
- Both can involve freedom: choosing what to consume, or choosing not to be governed by a desire or constraint.
- Both may involve resources, comfort, opportunity and the conditions of everyday life.
- Both can express agency rather than passivity.

**Where they may diverge — tentative**
- Consumer / Pig may emphasize *engagement with what is available*: appetite, enjoyment, acquisition, consumption, gratification, comfort or the pursuit of experiences.
- Liberated / Eagle may emphasize *freedom of direction and choice*: autonomy, release from constraints, independence, perspective and the ability to refuse an imposed path.

**Example of overlap**
A person chooses an experience because it brings pleasure, but also consciously decides what is worth pursuing and what is not. Desire and autonomy are both present.

**Potential false distinction to avoid**
Do not equate consumption with greed or liberation with rejecting all pleasure and material things. A person can freely choose enjoyment; a person can also become constrained by the pursuit of freedom itself.

**Pair question**
Is the intended tension consumption versus freedom, dependence versus autonomy, attachment versus release, or something else? These are related but not identical.

**Current assessment**
Pig and Eagle are visually legible symbols. “Consumer” is broad but can sound economically narrow; “Liberated” describes a state or outcome. Clarify the underlying patterns before renaming either.

### Pair 4 — The Follower / Sheep ↔ The Architect / Spider

**Evidence status**
Names and animals are inherited working material. The structural contrast below is a hypothesis, not owner-confirmed.

**Where they mirror**
- Both may relate to systems, groups, rules and structures.
- Both may require attention to relationships between parts.
- Both can support coordination and continuity.
- Both can be constructive: a system needs participants as well as people who design it.
- Both can involve trust, responsibility and long-term consequences.

**Where they may diverge — tentative**
- Follower / Sheep may emphasize *participating in, learning from, trusting or moving within an existing structure*: following guidance, shared practice, collective coordination, belonging and continuity.
- Architect / Spider may emphasize *creating, arranging or redesigning the structure itself*: planning, connecting elements, designing processes, building networks and shaping conditions.

**Example of overlap**
A team member follows an agreed process while also improving the process and helping redesign it. Both orientations can be present in one person and one project.

**Potential false distinction to avoid**
Do not define Follower as passive or unintelligent, or Architect as always independent and in control. Following can be deliberate and wise; designing can depend on collaboration and inherited knowledge.

**Animal fit**
Sheep is recognizable for collective movement and flock behaviour, but the stereotype of stupidity must be avoided. Spider is a strong visual metaphor for webs, networks and construction, but should not reduce Architect to literal building.

**Current assessment**
This is currently the clearest candidate structural contrast: working within / participating in structures versus creating / shaping structures. It remains provisional until the owner confirms that this captures the intended meanings.

### Pair 5 — The Performer / Peacock ↔ Transformation archetype / animal TBD

**Owner-grounded starting point**
The owner explicitly confirmed that Performer and the transformation concept are opposites. The transformation concept involves internal limitations, cumulative learning and evolution, integrating rather than erasing the past, and perspective shifts that reveal new possibilities. Transformation is not necessarily moral improvement or a superior endpoint.

**Where they mirror**
- Both may involve identity and how it changes or is experienced.
- Both can involve expression and interpretation.
- Both may be creative and intentional.
- Both can affect how a person relates to others.
- Both can be present together: internal change can alter expression; performance can become part of learning and change.

**Where they may diverge — tentative**
- Performer / Peacock may emphasize *making something visible*: expression, presentation, communication, display, performance, creativity and audience-facing presence.
- Transformation may emphasize *changing the internal frame or accumulated way of being*: learning, integration, reframing, adaptation, maturation and discovering a new possibility without erasing what came before.

**Example of overlap**
A person changes their understanding through experience and then expresses that change through clothing, art, speech or public performance. The inner evolution and outward expression are connected.

**Potential false distinction to avoid**
Do not claim performers do not evolve or that transformation must be invisible. The contrast concerns emphasis, not separate kinds of people or mutually exclusive processes.

**Animal / naming question**
Peacock is a strong symbol for display and expression. The transformation animal and name remain undecided. “The Becoming” is a candidate only; butterfly remains a legacy metaphor, not a decision.

**Current assessment**
Opposition is owner-confirmed. The proposed outward-expression / cumulative-inner-change axis is a working interpretation that needs owner testing.

### Pair 6 — The Predator / Wolf ↔ The Collector / Raven or Crow

**Evidence status**
Names and animals are inherited working material. The contrast below is exploratory.

**Where they mirror**
- Both may involve resources and value.
- Both may require attention, memory, timing and opportunity recognition.
- Both may involve persistence and strategy.
- Both may acquire or secure something that matters.
- Both may be protective or purposeful as well as self-interested.

**Where they may diverge — tentative**
- Predator / Wolf may emphasize *pursuit*: identifying a target or objective, tracking it, committing energy, timing an approach and acting to obtain it.
- Collector / Raven-Crow may emphasize *gathering and keeping*: noticing value, accumulating, preserving, curating, remembering or assembling resources and information over time.

**Example of overlap**
Someone pursues a specific opportunity and then preserves the knowledge, contacts or resources gained from it. Pursuit and collection may occur in sequence or together.

**Potential false distinction to avoid**
Do not reduce Wolf to aggression or Raven/Crow to hoarding. Pursuit can be protective or goal-directed; collecting can be thoughtful, useful and creative.

**Animal / naming question**
Wolf symbolism includes cooperation, social bonds and protection, not only predation. “Predator” may be too narrow or morally loaded. Raven versus Crow should be selected for the actual intended visual and symbolic qualities, not a simplistic shiny-object cliché.

**Current assessment**
A potentially useful pursuit / gathering distinction exists, but it needs a sharper owner-grounded rationale. The name Predator is especially worth reopening.

## 4. Where the archetypes must not be confused

These distinctions are not hard borders; they are useful questions that prevent the system from collapsing into a handful of generic traits.

- **Fox vs Owl:** Is the recognizable emphasis on astuteness in navigating a situation, or on the depth / breadth of knowledge being brought to it? Both may be intelligent.
- **Lion vs Peacock:** Is the emphasis on selfhood, dignity and assertion, or on expressing / presenting something to an audience? Both may seek recognition.
- **Lion vs Echo:** Is the emphasis on asserting one's own position, or on receiving and reflecting external signals? Both are relational.
- **Echo vs Follower:** Is the emphasis on mirroring / resonance, or on following / participating in a direction or group? A person may do both.
- **Pig vs Raven/Crow:** Is the emphasis on consuming / using what is acquired, or on gathering / preserving it? The same person may acquire, enjoy and keep.
- **Pig vs Wolf:** Is the emphasis on satisfying appetite or desire, or on pursuing a specific objective? Both may seek resources.
- **Eagle vs Sheep:** Is the emphasis on autonomy and choosing one's direction, or on belonging and coordinated participation? Both can be chosen consciously.
- **Sheep vs Spider:** Is the emphasis on participating in a structure, or shaping / constructing it? A person can alternate between these roles.
- **Spider vs Transformation:** Is the emphasis on designing an external system, or changing one's internal frame and integrating experience? The two can reinforce one another.
- **Peacock vs Transformation:** Is the emphasis on visible expression, or cumulative internal evolution? Each can influence the other.
- **Wolf vs Fox:** Is the emphasis on sustained pursuit and commitment to an objective, or on adaptable astuteness in navigating changing circumstances? Both may be strategic.
- **Wolf vs Raven/Crow:** Is the emphasis on pursuing and obtaining, or gathering and preserving? They may form a sequence rather than an opposition.

## 5. Important system-level observation

The six pairs do not all express the same type of contrast:
- Pair 1 may be a difference in emphasis within intelligence and knowledge.
- Pair 2 may concern self-reference and social reflection.
- Pair 3 may concern desire, resources and autonomy.
- Pair 4 may concern participation in structures and construction of structures.
- Pair 5 may concern outward expression and cumulative inner change.
- Pair 6 may concern pursuit and gathering.

This variation is not automatically a flaw. It may reflect the breadth of human experience. But if the pairs are presented as a formal six-pair architecture, each relationship must be meaningful and understandable. We should not force a single formula across all six.

2. **Pair 2:** determine what Echo means in the owner's original intent before changing the Deer or the name.
3. **Pair 3:** clarify whether the opposition concerns consumption versus autonomy, desire versus freedom, or a different axis.
4. **Pair 4:** test whether “participating in a structure” versus “creating a structure” matches the original Follower / Architect intent.
5. **Pair 5:** retain the owner-confirmed opposition; test the outward-expression versus cumulative-change interpretation without turning it into a binary.
6. **Pair 6:** clarify whether Predator means pursuit, hunting, instinct, obtaining, or something else; distinguish it from Collector without moralizing.

## 7. Conclusion

The twelve mirror one another because they share human qualities. They diverge through emphasis, orientation, context and the way those qualities are expressed. A person can therefore manifest one, several or all twelve without contradiction.

The goal is not to give each archetype exclusive territory. It is to make each one recognizable while preserving the connections that make the system human.
