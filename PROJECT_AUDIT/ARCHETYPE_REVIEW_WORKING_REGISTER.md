# Instinct 13 — Manifestation Review Working Register

**Status:** Working document; non-normative until reviewed and approved by the project owner.  
**Purpose:** Review the twelve conceptual positions and their inherited labels, pairings and animal symbols without treating legacy material as unquestionable canon.

## 1. Terminology distinction: archetype vs. manifestation

The project owner has clarified that **Archetype** and **Manifestation** must be distinguished; they are not interchangeable terms.

For the purposes of this review, the working distinction is:

- **Archetype:** an underlying recurring conceptual pattern or structure. It describes *what pattern is being considered*.
- **Manifestation:** the concrete, contextual expression through which a pattern becomes observable — in behaviour, choices, reactions, relationships or other forms. It describes *how, when and in what context the pattern appears*.

Consequences of this distinction:
- A person is not an archetype and must not be permanently classified by one.
- The same person may express different archetypal patterns in different contexts or at different times.
- One archetype may have multiple manifestations; manifestations can vary as context, awareness and choices change.
- An animal is neither the archetype nor its manifestation. It is a symbolic representation that may help communicate the archetype.
- Reviewing an archetype means reviewing its underlying concept, not merely changing its animal or describing one observed behaviour.

### Relationship to the existing Decision Log

The Decision Log dated 2026-08-05 records: **“Approved term 'Manifestation' replacing 'Archetype'.”** That entry now appears potentially ambiguous in light of the owner's clarification.

Do not silently delete or rewrite the historical decision. It must be revisited explicitly to determine whether it intended:
1. to replace the word *Archetype* throughout the entire conceptual model; or
2. to distinguish the observable expressions (*Manifestations*) from the underlying archetypes, while retaining both terms for different layers.

Until the owner resolves the historical scope, this register uses both terms according to the working distinction above and marks the constitutional terminology decision as requiring reconciliation.

## 2. The 13th: separate and exceptional use

The 13th is described in the canonical correction as **pure manifestation**, outside the twelve.

It is not a thirteenth archetype, animal, personality, character or fixed identity. It must remain nameless and formless.

This use of “pure manifestation” is a specific constitutional concept and must not be casually conflated with ordinary manifestations (the observable expressions of archetypes). The exact philosophical relationship between the 13th and the ordinary archetype/manifestation distinction remains subject to owner review.

## 3. Review order

For each opposing pair, work in this order:

1. **Archetype concept:** What recurring human pattern does it represent? Describe it without moral ranking or permanently classifying people.
2. **Opposition:** What is the meaningful conceptual tension between the two archetypes?
3. **Name:** Choose a concise name that expresses the concept. Do not use the animal's name as a substitute.
4. **Manifestations:** Identify how the pattern may appear in different contexts. Avoid treating one behaviour as the whole archetype.
5. **Animal / symbol:** Select an animal only after the concept is clear. Treat it as a replaceable metaphor.
6. **Visual language:** Define visual representation only after the conceptual work.
7. **Decision record:** Record rationale, alternatives, owner approval, date and version in the Decision Log.

## 4. Review principles

- Archetypes are conceptual patterns; manifestations are their contextual expressions.
- Neither term should be used as a permanent label for a person.
- An animal is a symbolic representation, not the archetype itself.
- Names may change when they misrepresent the concept.
- Pairings may change if the conceptual opposition does not hold.
- No side is inherently better, more evolved or morally superior to its opposite.
- Avoid definitions that assume a fixed endpoint or universal human classification.
- Preserve uncertainty explicitly; do not fill gaps by inference.
- Do not force a relationship between the twelve archetypes and the separate DQD dimensions.
- The 13th remains outside the twelve: formless, nameless and not an archetype or animal.

## 6. Pair 5 — current working notes

The project owner confirmed that The Performer and the transformation archetype are opposites.

The transformation concept, as currently described by the project owner:
- limitations are often internal to the individual;
- learning and experience produce continuous evolution;
- transformation does not erase the past or rebuild from absolute zero; it incorporates and reorganizes what came before;
- a change of perspective can reveal a different meaning or possibility without requiring external reality to change;
- transformation is not necessarily moral improvement, a superior state, or a predetermined final form.

**The Becoming** is a candidate name only. It is not approved as final. The animal symbol remains undecided. The butterfly is not the archetype's name and is not the 13th manifestation.
