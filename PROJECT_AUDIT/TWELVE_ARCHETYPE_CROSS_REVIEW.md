# Instinct 13 — Twelve Archetypes: Cross-Review and Revision Recommendations

**Status:** Exploratory cross-review; no new names, animal symbols or pairings are approved by this document.  
**Branch:** `docs/restore-canonical-i13-sources`  
**Purpose:** Complete a first-pass review of all twelve inherited archetype positions, identify semantic mismatches and overlaps, and recommend what should be retained, questioned or potentially changed. This is an identification instrument, not a set of closed definitions.

## 1. Owner-confirmed principles governing this review

The project owner has clarified:

- The twelve archetypes represent dimensions / recurring patterns of the human experience. They are not personality types into which people should be classified.
- At least one of the twelve is always present in human expression; multiple archetypes may be present together, and expressions may alternate.
- The same quality may appear through more than one archetype. Overlap is expected, not an error.
- The purpose is to identify what manifests, not to define each archetype exhaustively.
- A person is the 13 in the sense that the 13 represents human manifestation through one, several or all of the twelve. It is not necessary for all twelve to be expressed simultaneously.
- The original brand concept is visual and satirical / observational: twelve animals appear in human situations (examples supplied by the owner: a pig eating fast food, an owl at the stock exchange, a sheep holding a phone). Each edition presents the twelve; the 13th is not shown or revealed as an individual animal or character.
- The twelve animals are symbolic representations, not literal definitions of the archetypes.

**Terminology note:** Earlier canonical material described the 13th as a pure manifestation outside the twelve, nameless and formless. The owner's latest clarification adds that each person is the 13 and that the 13 manifests through one, several or all of the twelve. These statements require a deliberate reconciliation in the canonical document. This review records the newer clarification but does not silently rewrite constitutional material.

## 2. Executive assessment

The twelve-position structure is viable as a creative and symbolic system, but the inherited names do not all communicate the same kind of thing. Some are actions or roles (Manipulator, Follower, Performer, Collector), some are psychological concepts (Ego), some describe a condition (Liberated), and some describe a relationship or function (Echo, Architect). This asymmetry is not automatically wrong for a fashion brand, but it creates ambiguity when the twelve are presented as a coherent archetypal system.

The strongest current animal/name associations are:
- Pig — Consumer (clear visual shorthand; owner supplied the fast-food image).
- Sheep — Follower (recognizable collective-following symbolism; must not be reduced to stupidity).
- Spider — Architect (web-building gives a strong visual metaphor for construction and systems).
- Peacock — Performer (display, visibility and expressive presence).
- Eagle — Liberated (freedom, elevation and independence).
- Owl — Scholar (knowledge and observation; the owner's “owl that knows everything” makes the knowledge association explicit).

The most important potential mismatches are:
- Fox — The Manipulator: the animal can signify positive astuteness and cunning, while “Manipulator” strongly implies covert control or exploitation.
- Deer / Stag — The Echo: the animal does not immediately communicate echo, mirroring or repetition. The label and animal need to be reconciled.
- Lion — The Ego: the animal is clear for presence, pride and authority, but “Ego” is psychologically loaded and can be mistaken for selfishness or vanity only.
- Wolf — The Predator: wolf symbolism includes cooperation, loyalty, social bonds and protection, not only predation. “Predator” narrows and moralizes the animal.
- Raven / Crow — The Collector: promising, but the exact bird should be selected deliberately and the intended kind of collecting clarified.
- Transformation position: the underlying concept has owner-confirmed direction, but the name and animal are still unresolved. “The Becoming” is only a candidate. Butterfly is a legacy metaphor, not an approved symbol.

**Recommendation:** Do not replace the twelve-animal structure. Keep the current twelve positions as the working set, but reopen five naming/symbol decisions and one animal decision. Avoid approving a new name based only on this audit.

## 3. Cross-review of all twelve positions

### 1. The Manipulator — Fox

**Owner-grounded signals**
- Astuteness / cunning.
- Cunning may be constructive, beneficial or protective; it is not inherently negative.

**Additional qualities to investigate**
- Resourcefulness, adaptability, situational intelligence, alertness, timing, practical ingenuity, reading circumstances, improvisation, finding indirect routes, diplomacy, discretion, anticipating consequences and recognizing opportunities.

**Possible manifestations**
- Finding a clever solution under constraints; adapting a plan when circumstances change; using tact to navigate a difficult conversation; noticing an opportunity others overlook; protecting someone through discretion.

**Overlap**
- Owl: observation, intelligence, anticipation.
- Spider: strategy and planning.
- Wolf: timing and pursuit.
- Eagle: independent thinking.

**Review finding**
The Fox is a strong symbolic fit for astuteness. “The Manipulator” is the weak point: it turns a broad and potentially constructive pattern into a morally charged tactic. It may make the audience assume deception, coercion or exploitation, which conflicts with the owner's correction that Fox-like cunning can be good.

**Recommendation: REOPEN THE NAME; RETAIN FOX PROVISIONALLY.**
Candidate directions for investigation, not approvals: “The Strategist”, “The Tactician”, “The Resourceful” or another owner-originated term. “Strategist” may be too narrow if the intended scope includes playful cleverness, adaptability and everyday ingenuity. Do not select a replacement yet.

### 2. The Scholar — Owl

**Owner-grounded signals**
- Profound / encompassing knowledge, symbolically described by the owner as “the owl that knows everything”.

**Additional qualities to investigate**
- Wisdom, learning, intellectual curiosity, memory, synthesis, discernment, insight, broad perspective, depth, pattern recognition, research, contemplation, understanding context and connecting distant pieces of knowledge.

**Possible manifestations**
- Integrating knowledge from different fields; recognizing a pattern in a complex situation; teaching from accumulated experience; seeing historical context; understanding implications beyond the immediate event.

**Overlap**
- Fox: intelligence, observation and anticipation.
- Raven: memory, curiosity and gathering information.
- Spider: systems thinking.
- Transformation: learning and integrating experience.

**Review finding**
Owl is a strong and owner-grounded symbol. “Scholar” communicates learning and study, but may be narrower or more institutional than the owner's image of encompassing knowledge. It can imply academic specialization rather than broad wisdom.

**Recommendation: RETAIN OWL; REOPEN THE NAME FOR SEMANTIC TESTING.**
“The Sage” is a candidate direction, not an approved replacement. “Scholar” may still be right if learning, inquiry and accumulated knowledge are central. The key is to distinguish deep knowledge from mere possession of information without making the Owl infallible.

### 3. The Ego — Lion

**Owner-grounded status**
- The current name and Lion symbol are inherited working material; no detailed owner-authored definition or pair rationale is recorded.

**Qualities to investigate**
- Selfhood, identity, self-respect, pride, dignity, confidence, presence, agency, ambition, assertiveness, authority, recognition, personal boundaries, courage, leadership and the need to be seen or respected.

**Possible manifestations**
- Taking responsibility publicly; defending a boundary; seeking recognition for work; asserting one's position; leading under pressure; protecting dignity; responding strongly to disrespect.

**Overlap**
- Peacock: visibility and recognition.
- Eagle: autonomy and self-determination.
- Wolf: leadership and territoriality.
- Echo: identity in relation to others and external reflection.

**Review finding**
Lion is a strong visual symbol for presence, authority, courage and pride. “Ego” is ambiguous: in everyday language it often means vanity or self-importance; in psychology it has more technical meanings. It risks making a broad human dimension look inherently negative.

**Recommendation: RETAIN LION PROVISIONALLY; REOPEN THE NAME AND THE PAIR RATIONALE.**
Possible name directions to test, not approvals: “The Self”, “The Identity”, “The Sovereign” or retaining “The Ego” with a deliberately broad interpretation. The choice depends on whether the intended center is selfhood, pride, status, self-assertion or recognition.

### 4. The Echo — Stag / Deer

**Owner-grounded status**
- The inherited label is Echo and the current working animal is Stag / Deer. No detailed owner-authored definition or opposition rationale is recorded.

**Qualities to investigate**
- Resonance, reflection, mirroring, responsiveness, sensitivity to social signals, empathy, listening, imitation, belonging, attunement, emotional receptivity, recognition of others and the way external voices or environments are internalized.

**Possible manifestations**
- Reflecting the language or mood of a group; adapting to social cues; carrying another person's words or influence; responding sensitively to an environment; seeking belonging; repeating a message that resonates.

**Overlap**
- Follower: belonging and group influence.
- Performer: response to an audience.
- Owl: listening and perception.
- Transformation: incorporating external experience.

**Review finding**
“Echo” can be a powerful concept if it means resonance, reflection, repetition or the way a person carries and returns what they receive. The Deer / Stag does not immediately communicate those meanings. Deer can communicate alertness, sensitivity, grace, vulnerability or herd awareness, but those are not automatically “Echo”.

**Recommendation: REOPEN THE ANIMAL / NAME MATCH; DO NOT APPROVE STAG OR DEER YET.**
Two directions to investigate:
- Keep “Echo” and test an animal whose visible behaviour strongly communicates mimicry, repetition or vocal echo (the parrot is a plausible candidate, not a decision).
- Keep Deer / Stag and investigate whether the intended archetype is actually sensitivity, resonance, attunement or alertness rather than echo in the literal sense.
Do not collapse “Echo” into “Follower”: mirroring and following may overlap but are not identical.

### 5. The Consumer — Pig

**Owner-grounded visual reference**
- The owner proposed a pig eating fast food as an example of the original brand concept.

**Qualities to investigate**
- Appetite, desire, consumption, acquisition, gratification, comfort-seeking, sensory pleasure, abundance, convenience, reward-seeking, resource use, spending, collecting experiences, impulse and the relationship with excess or moderation.

**Possible manifestations**
- Eating for pleasure or comfort; buying for status or convenience; consuming content compulsively; seeking constant novelty; enjoying abundance; choosing immediate reward over delayed benefit.

**Overlap**
- Collector: acquisition and accumulation.
- Performer: consumption as display or status.
- Predator: taking / pursuing resources.
- Liberated: freedom from compulsive consumption or from imposed scarcity.

**Review finding**
Pig is an immediately legible, visually rich symbol for appetite and consumption, especially in satirical human situations. The risk is reducing the archetype to gluttony or treating the animal as an insult. “Consumer” is broad enough to cover material, digital, sensory and experiential consumption, but should not be reduced to shopping or food.

**Recommendation: RETAIN CONSUMER / PIG PROVISIONALLY.**
Keep the visual satire, but allow constructive, neutral and excessive forms of consumption. Do not imply that every consumer expression is vice or weakness.

### 6. The Liberated — Eagle

**Owner-grounded status**
- The inherited name and animal are present; the broader meaning is not yet fully reviewed with the owner.

**Qualities to investigate**
- Freedom, autonomy, independence, self-determination, release from constraints, nonconformity, exploration, perspective, choice, self-reliance, openness, breaking patterns and responsibility for one's own direction.

**Possible manifestations**
- Leaving a limiting structure; questioning a rule; choosing an unconventional path; gaining distance from social pressure; setting one's own direction; reclaiming agency.

**Overlap**
- Fox: adaptability and independent problem-solving.
- Lion: agency and self-assertion.
- Transformation: changing one's perspective.
- Follower: the tension between belonging and autonomy.

**Review finding**
Eagle is a strong symbol for freedom, perspective and elevation. “Liberated” describes a condition or outcome more than a recurring pattern, and its exact relationship to Consumer is not self-evident. The contrast could concern dependence / appetite versus autonomy, but that axis must not be assumed.

**Recommendation: RETAIN EAGLE PROVISIONALLY; REOPEN THE NAME AND PAIR AXIS.**
Test whether the intended center is freedom, autonomy, release, independence or emancipation. “The Free” and “The Independent” are possible semantic directions, not approvals.

### 7. The Follower — Sheep

**Owner-grounded status**
- The inherited label and animal are present; no detailed owner-authored definition or opposition rationale is recorded.

**Qualities to investigate**
- Belonging, trust, cooperation, coordination, loyalty, social learning, imitation, acceptance of guidance, shared norms, tradition, safety in numbers, collective identity, participation and conformity.

**Possible manifestations**
- Following a trusted guide; adopting a group's practice; coordinating with a team; preserving a tradition; accepting a social norm; repeating a trend without examining it.

**Overlap**
- Echo: mirroring and social resonance.
- Architect: following an established system or process.
- Deer: sensitivity to group and environmental cues.
- Liberated: tension between belonging and independence.

**Review finding**
Sheep is an immediately recognizable symbol for following a group, but it carries a strong stereotype of stupidity and passive obedience. The archetype must include positive forms such as trust, cooperation, social learning and solidarity. Otherwise it becomes a caricature rather than a human pattern.

**Recommendation: RETAIN SHEEP; RETAIN FOLLOWER PROVISIONALLY, WITH A BROADER CONCEPTUAL RANGE.**
The important question is not “is following bad?” but what makes someone follow, what they follow, and when following is chosen, useful, habitual or imposed.

### 8. The Architect — Spider

**Owner-grounded status**
- The inherited label and animal are present; no detailed owner-authored definition or opposition rationale is recorded.

**Qualities to investigate**
- Construction, design, planning, systems thinking, organization, interconnection, patience, precision, structural creativity, sequencing, long-term thinking, process design, resource allocation and building conditions for outcomes.

**Possible manifestations**
- Designing a process; connecting people or resources; building a network; creating a system; planning several steps ahead; making an idea operational; maintaining a structure over time.

**Overlap**
- Fox: strategy and resourcefulness.
- Owl: systems understanding.
- Collector: assembling components or information.
- Transformation: redesign and iterative improvement.

**Review finding**
Spider is a strong visual metaphor for construction, networks, interdependence and patient design. “Architect” is understandable, but it may imply only buildings or formal professions. The archetype should include anyone who creates structures, systems or connected arrangements.

**Recommendation: RETAIN ARCHITECT / SPIDER PROVISIONALLY.**
The pair with Follower has a plausible tension — adopting / operating within an existing structure versus designing / constructing one — but this is only a candidate axis, not an owner-approved definition.

### 9. The Performer — Peacock

**Owner-grounded status**
- The owner explicitly confirmed that Performer is opposed to the transformation concept in Pair 5.

**Qualities to investigate**
- Expression, visibility, presentation, communication, display, creativity, performance, aesthetic awareness, charisma, audience awareness, theatricality, identity presentation, influence through expression and desire to make an impression.

**Possible manifestations**
- Presenting an idea; dressing to communicate identity; performing on stage; curating a public image; using humour or drama to communicate; seeking attention; making work visible.

**Overlap**
- Ego: recognition, pride and presence.
- Echo: audience response and social feedback.
- Consumer: status and display through possessions.
- Transformation: changing how one expresses oneself over time.

**Review finding**
Peacock is a strong visual symbol for display, beauty, visibility and expressive presence. Performer is broader than vanity and should include genuine artistic expression, communication and play. Its confirmed opposition to transformation should be interpreted carefully: outward expression versus inward / cumulative evolution is a possible tension, not a claim that performers cannot transform.

**Recommendation: RETAIN PERFORMER / PEACOCK PROVISIONALLY.**
The opposition is owner-confirmed; the exact conceptual axis still needs to be articulated without turning external expression and internal change into mutually exclusive human states.

### 10. Transformation archetype — animal TBD

**Owner-grounded concept**
- Limitations are often internal.
- Learning and experience produce continuous evolution.
- Transformation is cumulative: it does not destroy the past or rebuild from zero.
- A change of perspective can reveal new meaning or possibility even when external reality has not changed.
- Transformation is not necessarily moral improvement or a superior endpoint.
- The owner confirmed this concept as the opposite of The Performer.

**Qualities to investigate**
- Learning, cumulative growth, integration, adaptation, reframing, renewal, perspective change, self-awareness, flexibility, maturation, reorganization, reinterpretation, resilience and discovering possibilities.

**Possible manifestations**
- Revising a belief after new evidence; integrating a difficult experience; changing one's approach without denying one's past; seeing a familiar situation differently; adding capability through practice; evolving a personal narrative.

**Overlap**
- Owl: learning and knowledge.
- Fox: adaptation.
- Eagle: release from limiting frames.
- Architect: redesign and iterative construction.
- All twelve: any archetype can change in how it is expressed.

**Review finding**
The concept is coherent and owner-grounded, but the name and animal are not settled. “The Becoming” is a candidate only. Butterfly is a legacy metaphor, but literal butterfly metamorphosis can suggest a total discontinuity or a dramatic before/after transformation, which may not communicate cumulative development. It can still work symbolically if the intended reading is carefully considered; it should not be retained merely because it is familiar.

**Recommendation: KEEP THE CONCEPT; DO NOT APPROVE THE NAME OR ANIMAL YET.**
Investigate animals or visual metaphors that communicate growth through accumulated stages, adaptation and integration rather than destruction / rebirth. The animal should be selected after the concept and name are stable.

### 11. The Predator — Wolf

**Owner-grounded status**
- The inherited label and animal are present; no detailed owner-authored definition or opposition rationale is recorded.

**Qualities to investigate**
- Pursuit, focus, instinct, initiative, hunting, determination, patience, timing, survival, target orientation, competitive drive, tactical action, protection and coordinated pursuit.

**Possible manifestations**
- Pursuing a goal persistently; recognizing and acting on an opportunity; protecting a group; tracking a problem to its source; waiting for the right moment; mobilizing coordinated effort.

**Overlap**
- Fox: tactical intelligence and timing.
- Lion: dominance and courage.
- Collector: acquisition and resource gathering.
- Follower: pack coordination and group loyalty.

**Review finding**
Wolf is a rich animal symbol: social bonds, cooperation, communication, territoriality, endurance and hunting. “Predator” narrows the concept to taking / attacking and can imply moral aggression. It may also overlap with Consumer and Collector unless the underlying pattern is clarified.

**Recommendation: REOPEN THE NAME; RETAIN WOLF PROVISIONALLY.**
“The Hunter” or “The Pursuer” are candidate directions, not approvals. “Hunter” may create a clearer contrast with Collector / Gatherer, but this must be tested against the intended human qualities and the owner's original intent.

### 12. The Collector — Raven / Crow

**Owner-grounded status**
- The inherited label is Collector; the current working symbol is Raven / Crow. No exact bird or detailed owner-authored definition has been confirmed.

**Qualities to investigate**
- Gathering, collecting, preserving, memory, curiosity, curation, noticing overlooked value, accumulation, resourcefulness, archiving, association, keeping, categorizing and assembling a personal repertoire.

**Possible manifestations**
- Saving useful objects; collecting information; preserving memories; gathering references; curating images or ideas; keeping resources for future use; connecting collected fragments into new insights.

**Overlap**
- Consumer: acquisition and desire.
- Owl: knowledge and memory.
- Architect: assembling components into systems.
- Predator: obtaining resources.

**Review finding**
Raven / Crow can communicate intelligence, curiosity, object interaction, memory and resource gathering. But “Collector” needs a clear center: collecting objects, information, memories, experiences, resources or all of these as a broader pattern? The bird should not be selected only through the popular but unreliable cliché that ravens collect shiny things.

**Recommendation: RETAIN COLLECTOR PROVISIONALLY; CHOOSE RAVEN OR CROW DELIBERATELY.**
Crow may fit urban, everyday, resourceful collecting and caching; Raven may offer a more mythic, solitary and visually dramatic presence. These are art-direction hypotheses, not biological definitions or owner decisions.

## 4. Review of the six pairings

The pairings should be treated as tensions or complementary orientations, not mutually exclusive opposites. A person may express both sides at once or at different times.

| Pair | Current pairing | Candidate tension to investigate | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Manipulator / Fox ↔ Scholar / Owl | Situational astuteness ↔ depth / breadth of knowledge | Keep pairing under review. Owner rejected the earlier control-versus-understanding axis. Name “Manipulator” is likely misleading. |
| 2 | Ego / Lion ↔ Echo / Deer | Self-reference / assertion ↔ reflection / resonance with others | Potentially meaningful, but “Echo” and Deer do not yet align clearly. Reopen animal or name. |
| 3 | Consumer / Pig ↔ Liberated / Eagle | Desire / consumption / dependence ↔ autonomy / freedom | Plausible tension, but not yet owner-grounded. Names are semantically asymmetric. |
| 4 | Follower / Sheep ↔ Architect / Spider | Adopting / participating in existing structures ↔ designing / constructing structures | Strongest candidate pair logic after visual-symbol review; keep provisional. |
| 5 | Performer / Peacock ↔ Transformation / TBD | Outward expression / presentation ↔ inward cumulative evolution / reframing | Opposition itself is owner-confirmed; exact axis, name and animal remain open. |
| 6 | Predator / Wolf ↔ Collector / Raven or Crow | Pursuing / obtaining ↔ gathering / preserving | Plausible but needs sharper distinction; “Predator” may be unnecessarily negative. |

**Cross-pair finding:** the six pairs are not equally coherent yet. Pair 4 has a plausible structural tension; Pair 5 has owner-confirmed opposition but needs careful wording; Pairs 1, 2, 3 and 6 need more conceptual work. Do not force all pairs into identical kinds of opposition.

## 5. Cross-archetype overlaps to preserve

These overlaps are expected and should not be “fixed” by assigning each quality to only one archetype.

- **Intelligence / perception:** Fox, Owl, Raven/Crow, Spider, Wolf.
- **Adaptation / change:** Fox, Liberated, Transformation, Follower.
- **Identity / visibility / recognition:** Ego, Performer, Echo.
- **Belonging / social influence:** Follower, Echo, Wolf, Performer.
- **Acquisition / resources:** Consumer, Collector, Predator.
- **Knowledge / memory / learning:** Owl, Collector, Transformation.
- **Construction / creation:** Architect, Performer, Transformation.
- **Autonomy / agency:** Liberated, Ego, Fox, Wolf.
- **Pleasure / reward / desire:** Consumer, Performer, Collector.
- **Protection / security:** Fox, Lion, Sheep, Wolf, Collector.

A quality appearing in several archetypes does not make them duplicates. The investigation must examine differences in emphasis, context, intention, relationship and expression without imposing exclusivity.

## 6. Recommended revision decisions

### High-priority review
1. **The Manipulator → reopen name.** Fox's owner-grounded astuteness can be positive; “Manipulator” carries a negative meaning that may distort it.
2. **The Echo ↔ Stag / Deer → reopen the match.** Decide whether Echo is truly about mirroring / resonance or whether the Deer points to a different concept. Parrot is a candidate if Echo remains.
3. **The Ego → reopen semantic scope.** Confirm whether the intended center is selfhood, pride, authority, recognition or self-assertion.
4. **The Predator → reopen name.** Wolf represents more than predation; “Hunter” / “Pursuer” may better capture pursuit without moralizing.
5. **Transformation → keep concept, leave name and animal open.** Do not approve butterfly or “The Becoming” by inheritance or convenience.

### Medium-priority review
6. **The Scholar → test “Scholar” against “Sage”.** Owl's knowledge symbolism may be broader than academic study.
7. **The Liberated → clarify the pattern.** Freedom, autonomy and release are related but not identical; determine which is central.
8. **The Collector → choose Raven or Crow and clarify the object of collecting.**
9. **The Consumer → retain Pig, but broaden beyond food and avoid reducing it to gluttony.**
10. **The Follower → retain Sheep, but include positive and intentional forms of following.**
11. **The Architect → retain Spider; clarify that architecture means systems and structures, not only buildings.**
12. **The Performer → retain Peacock; include authentic expression, creativity and communication, not only attention-seeking.**

## 7. What should not change

- Do not change the twelve-position structure.
- Do not turn archetypes into fixed personality types.
- Do not assign exclusive qualities to individual archetypes.
- Do not impose good / bad, weak / strong or evolved / unevolved rankings.
- Do not force every pair to be a strict binary.
- Do not replace the original visual idea: animals in human situations.
- Do not reveal the 13th as an additional animal or character.
- Do not treat the 13th as a thirteenth archetype.
- Do not turn the current working recommendations into approved canon without the owner's explicit decision.

## 8. Overall conclusion

The twelve do not need twelve isolated, mutually exclusive definitions. They need twelve recognizable symbolic centers with open fields of qualities and manifestations, meaningful enough to inspire visual storytelling but flexible enough to overlap in real human experience.

The present set is worth preserving as a working structure. The main changes likely needed are **semantic corrections**, not a wholesale replacement of animals:
- Fox is stronger than “Manipulator” suggests.
- Deer / Stag is not yet convincingly connected to “Echo”.
- Lion may be broader than “Ego”.
- Wolf is broader than “Predator”.
- Transformation is conceptually promising but still lacks a settled name and animal.

No name, animal or pairing is approved by this audit. The next owner-led pass should focus first on the five high-priority issues above, one at a time, and record explicit decisions.
